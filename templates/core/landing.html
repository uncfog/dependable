{# ===== Paper against SCHOOLCORD ========================================== #}

Why paper registers and spreadsheets fail.

Notebooks and scattered WhatsApp messages lose money, start awkward arguments with parents and leave you blind when you are away from school.

The paper way

  • Unreported payments. Cash paid to a bursar, or a transfer, and nobody tells the proprietor.
  • Exam hall disputes. “I paid last month,” with no quick way to check.
  • Endless reconciliation. Weekends spent balancing ledger notebooks.
  • Lost reminders. Letters home that never leave the school bag.

The SCHOOLCORD way

  • One overview. Expected fees, confirmed cash and transfers, and what is still owed, as it happens.
  • Reminders in one tap. Polite, itemised reminders to parents' phones.
  • Receipts on the spot. A receipt for every payment, with an audit trail behind it.
  • Roles that protect you. The bursar records payments; the proprietor's controls stay locked.

{# ===== Questions ========================================================= #}

Answers for school proprietors.

Can I use SCHOOLCORD if my bursar is not good with computers? +

Yes. The screens use plain buttons and short forms. If your bursar can send a WhatsApp message, they can record a payment and issue a receipt after a few minutes of showing.

How does it help me when I am not at school? +

Sign in from any phone or tablet. You see payments as your bursar records them, so no money goes unreported.

What if the network is poor at our school? +

SCHOOLCORD uses very little data and works on 3G and 4G. It needs a connection to save an entry. Nothing is kept on the phone, so a lost phone does not lose or leak your records.

Can parents pay from the reminder? +

Reminders carry the child's balance and your school's bank details, so a parent can transfer straight from their banking app. You record the payment when it arrives.

How do I pay for a plan? +

From your school's settings, choose a plan and you get our bank details and a reference. Pay by transfer with that reference, tell us you have paid, and your plan switches on when the money arrives.
