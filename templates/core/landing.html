Light-only by design: `html_attrs` pins data-theme="light" and adds data-theme-lock, which ui.js respects, and `theme_init` drops the script that would otherwise follow a stored choice or the OS into dark mode. There is no theme switch on this page. Type is the design's own -- Plus Jakarta Sans and Playfair Display -- loaded in `fonts` and applied through `.lp-lattice` (assets/app.css), which re-points Tailwind's `font-sans` / `font-serif` inside this page only. The palette is the `lattice-*` colours in the same file. No CDN Tailwind: everything compiles into static/css/app.css like every other page. Every way in goes to a real route: Login to `login`, every "set up" / "create" / "get started" / plan button to `core:signup`. The WhatsApp links read `support_whatsapp_url` from apps/core/branding.py, and the footer prints the same line as `support_phone`. The "Test Drive" section is a client-side demo with made-up records. It is labelled as such, it contains no form, and its script never calls the server -- recording a payment only edits an array in the page, and "WhatsApp" shows the message it would send instead of opening WhatsApp to a stranger's number. The image slots are gradients only (`.lp-hero-bg`, `.lp-section-bg`, `.lp-cta-bg`): the design's stock photos are not licensed for use. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers for School Proprietors

CAN I USE SCHOOLCORD IF MY BURSAR IS NOT COMPUTER LITERATE? ↓

Yes! SCHOOLCORD is designed with simple buttons and straightforward forms. If your bursar can send a message on WhatsApp, they can easily record payments and issue e-receipts on SCHOOLCORD within 10 minutes of training.

HOW DOES SCHOOLCORD HELP ME WHEN I AM NOT AT SCHOOL? ↓

You can log into your Director Portal from any smartphone or tablet anywhere in the world. You see real-time updates as payments are entered by your bursar, ensuring zero unreported money.

WHAT HAPPENS IF INTERNET NETWORK IS POOR AT OUR SCHOOL LOCATION? ↓

SCHOOLCORD requires very little data and is optimized for 3G/4G Nigerian mobile networks. Entries can also be queued offline and automatically synced once connection is restored.

CAN PARENTS PAY THROUGH WHATSAPP REMINDERS? ↓

Yes! Reminders sent via WhatsApp include your verified school bank account details and payment breakdown, making it convenient for parents to transfer fees immediately from their mobile banking apps.
