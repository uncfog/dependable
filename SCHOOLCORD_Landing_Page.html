FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers for School Proprietors

CAN I USE SCHOOLCORD IF MY BURSAR IS NOT COMPUTER LITERATE? ↓

Yes! SCHOOLCORD is designed with simple buttons and straightforward forms. If your bursar can send a message on WhatsApp, they can easily record payments and issue e-receipts on SCHOOLCORD within 10 minutes of training.

HOW DOES SCHOOLCORD HELP ME WHEN I AM NOT AT SCHOOL? ↓

You can log into your Director Portal from any smartphone or tablet anywhere in the world. You see real-time updates as payments are entered by your bursar, ensuring zero unreported money.

WHAT HAPPENS IF INTERNET NETWORK IS POOR AT OUR SCHOOL LOCATION? ↓

SCHOOLCORD requires very little data and is optimized for 3G/4G Nigerian mobile networks. Entries can also be queued offline and automatically synced once connection is restored.

CAN PARENTS PAY THROUGH WHATSAPP REMINDERS? ↓

Yes! Reminders sent via WhatsApp include your verified school bank account details and payment breakdown, making it convenient for parents to transfer fees immediately from their mobile banking apps.
