The five most-used screens, on desktop and phone, in the style of the two references you sent: a blue header band and sidebar, lime for the one primary action and the active item, white rounded cards, coloured pill tags, and coloured tiles for quick access. Decisions

StyleThe two references: blue header band and sidebar, lime for the one action and the active item, white rounded cards, coloured pill tags, coloured quick-access tiles. This replaces the restrained direction in the earlier review.
Confirm nowUnticked by default. The button reads Save for checking.
Date paidA text field that takes dd/mm/yyyy, with a calendar button beside it.

Still open

Brand colourThe reference blue replaces the navy in the app. The wordmark stays. Confirm this is what you want.
RedUsed only for overdue balances. The reference's red "High" tag is not copied, because red would then mean priority rather than money.
Coloured tilesEach tile's colour marks the section it opens, not a status.
Not yet drawnClasses, subjects, branches, account settings, add student, payment detail, family payments, platform schools, sign-in and the locked pages. These follow once the direction is approved.
